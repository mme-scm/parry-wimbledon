# analysis/limitations.md

Issues raised by the adversarial review (review/critic_analysis_v1.md) that were not fixed by re-analysis, with the reason each stands. Fixed items are logged in analysis/formulas/plan.md (addendum 2) and analysis/metre/plan.md (post hoc addendum); the fixes are summarised at the end.

## Standing limitations

1. **The measure is repetition, not Parry's formula (critic B3, partly fixed).** "Repeated-n-gram coverage" counts tokens inside any word string that recurs; with n ≥ 2 it is a lexical-repetition rate dominated by "the first", "in the". The stricter family (n ≥ 3, n ≥ 4, content-only) is now reported alongside, and the shuffled-word baseline is subtracted for each definition, but no automatic definition captures Parry's "regularly employed under the same metrical conditions to express a given essential idea". The economy/extension tests and the noun-epithet inventory are the only places where function, not string identity, is the unit. Stands because no better operationalisation exists without hand annotation of every utterance; the paper must present coverage as a repetition statistic and the Duggan comparison as an analogy only.
2. **TV speech versus press-conference answers is indeterminate (critic A1, relabelled).** The press transcripts are edited stenography, the TV text is raw clip-cut ASR with unknown WER (lower bound 22 errors per 1,000 words), the TV formulas are identified cross-broadcaster and the press formulas within-genre. Injected substitution noise of 12.2% brings the press text to the TV level. The contrast is reported as indeterminate pending a WER estimate, which needs audio (FOR_HUMAN.md).
3. **TV speech versus written live text is a corpus contrast, not a medium effect (critic B7).** Different matches, outlet, period, ASR versus edited prose, and segmentation (clips versus updates). In the split-half design the sign is stable under every injected-noise level tried and under subsampling to matched size; in the held-out design the Cornell coverage at 15% injected substitution noise (55.6, 95% CI 53.6–57.9) overlaps the TV value (55.0) and the means cross near 16% noise, which is within the plausible range of the unknown ASR error rate. A difference between the two corpora as transcribed is established; its attribution to the spoken/written distinction is not, and even the direction is not secure against ASR noise in the held-out design (critic v2, M1).
4. **Null results on timing, thrift and extension are low-powered (critic B6, metre B5).** Minimum detectable effects at 80% power: formula coverage between dead-time terciles 6.4–7.2 points (formulas) and 5.3–6.5 points (metre T2); thrift θ 0.30–0.42; extension ρ 0.17–0.22; metre T3 ρ 0.066–0.081, T4 8.3–10.6 points. Clip-level text attributed to points and ASR errors both attenuate a true effect towards zero by an unknown factor. "No effect found" therefore means "no effect larger than these bounds, as measured".
5. **Thrift cells are clip-level and coarse.** "Context" is the dead-time tercile and the heuristic phase tag of the clip, not the position of the name inside the utterance; "slot" labels come from a parser that agreed with hand labels in 33 of 40 cases. Umpire-pattern references ("Mr X", "Game X") are now excluded in the primary run and kept in a sensitivity table.
6. **C3a used the interval before the clip's point (critic C3).** The clip's text runs from the rally into the following dead time, so C3b (time after) is the apt predictor; both are null. Label permutations among clips treat adjacent clips as exchangeable; the metre analysis uses circular shifts, and its shift-null SD is within 3–5% of the permutation-null SD, so serial correlation does not change the conclusion. Stands as a design note rather than being re-run under a new pre-registration.
7. **The 2023 replication is not a comparable-range replication (critic C6).** The 2023 video omits changeovers, so available time A spans 15–117 s against 14–305 s in 2019, and T4 (changeover contrast) is untestable there.
8. **Metre T1 (words track available time) is consistent with H0 (critic C4).** The within-game ρ is 0.166 [0.046, 0.280] against 0.248 overall; part of the overall correlation is between-category (changeover versus within-game) and presence-of-text. The result shows only that the transcript is tied to point timing.
9. **Single broadcaster, no speakers, no word times, no audio** (STATUS.md Phase 0). Commentator identity, medium and match are confounded; intonation units cannot be measured; H1 had to be re-specified with the serve-to-serve cycle as the frame.
10. **De-duplication rule.** Removing text repeated across consecutive clips (17.7% of words in 2019) deletes some true repetitions (e.g. a score call repeated by the commentator) along with window overlap; the sensitivity run on text_dedup versus text_corrected is in analysis/formulas/results/density_sensitivity.csv. The critic verified that no held-out boundary leaks through it.
